Best Movies According to Chris

My personal top 5 favorite movies

1

Lone Survivor (2015)

Based on a true story, this film follows the journey of a Navy SEAL team during a mission in Afghanistan.

2

The Terminal List (2022)

A gripping action thriller that delves into the life of a Navy SEAL who uncovers a conspiracy.

3

Jack Reacher (2012) & Jack Reacher: Never Go Back (2016)

A pair of action-packed films featuring Tom Cruise as the titular character, a former military police officer turned private investigator.

4

The Equalizer (2014) & The Equalizer 2 (2018)

Denzel Washington stars in these action thrillers as a retired intelligence officer.

5

Sherlock Holmes (2009) & Sherlock Holmes: A Game of Shadows (2011)

Robert Downey Jr. and Jude Law bring the iconic detective duo to life in these films.
