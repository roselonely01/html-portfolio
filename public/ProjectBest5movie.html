Best Movies According to Chris

My Top 5 Movies

1. LONE SURVIVOR (2015)

Based on a true story, this film follows the journey
of a Navy SEAL team during a mission in Afghanistan.

2. THE TERMINAL LIST (2022)

A gripping action thriller that delves into the life
of a Navy SEAL who uncovers a conspiracy.

3. JACK REACHER (2012) & JACK REACHER: NEVER GO BACK (2016)

A pair of action-packed films featuring Tom Cruise as the titular character,
a former military police officer turned private investigator.

4. THE EQUALIZER (2014) & THE EQUALIZER 2 (2018)

Denzel Washington stars in these action thrillers
as a retired intelligence officer

5. sherlock holmes (2009) & sherlock holmes: a game of shadows (2011)

Robert Downey Jr. and Jude Law bring
the iconic detective duo to life in these
